## Corridor operations
1. Preserve claim, original source URL, publication date, retrieval date, origin type and licensing notes.
2. When lawful copies are available, hash exact UTF-8 bytes (SHA-256), not just rendered pages.
3. Compare short authorized text samples: raw hash, normalized hash, and three-word overlap.
4. Label mechanical checks PASS/FAIL; attribution, intent, licensing, and uncertain dialogue UNKNOWN unless primary evidence resolves them.
5. Publish correction receipts append-only, retaining the original mistake and its dated correction.
6. Separate original songs, lyrics, transcripts, captions, news, satire, and analysis. Similarity does not prove plagiarism.
7. No wholesale publication of copyrighted Dylan lyrics and no unauthorized scraping.
8. Never publish private family data, keys, addresses, wallets, or protected identity information.
