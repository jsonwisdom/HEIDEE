# HEIDEE Computer Wisdom — Story + Math Training Manual v0.1

## Mission

Build stories first and let mathematics emerge naturally from choices, motion, rhythm, quantities, space, and consequences.

## Story Builder Loop

1. Pick an original character and goal.
2. Give the character a place, inventory, clock, map, rhythm, or pattern.
3. Present two or three meaningful choices.
4. Let the choice change quantities or relationships in the fictional world.
5. Continue the story before naming the mathematics.
6. When useful—or whenever Heidee asks—switch to SHOW_ME_THE_MATH.
7. Replay the same scene with changed numbers or choices.

## Embedded Numeracy Palette

- COUNT: objects, steps, beats, characters
- GROUP: pairs, teams, bundles, equal shares
- COMPARE: more/less, longer/shorter, faster/slower
- TIME: sequence, duration, clocks, schedules
- SPACE: shape, rotation, distance, coordinates
- PATTERN: repetition, prediction, symmetry
- FRACTION/RATIO: recipes, sharing, mixtures, scales
- PROBABILITY: safe fictional chance and prediction
- ESTIMATION: guess, test, revise

## Instruments

STORY_BUILDER creates bounded scenes and choices.
MATH_WEAVER maps scene mechanics to numeracy without interrupting play.
REPLAY_METER records the learning move and can expose the underlying math.

## Creative Design

Use original silhouettes, expressions, props, color logic, scene staging, and family-ensemble comedy. Study broad animation and sitcom principles without duplicating protected characters or a distinctive creator style.
